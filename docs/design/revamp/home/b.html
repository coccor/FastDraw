How a FastDraw diagram gets made

The diagram on the right is one file, shown at each step as you scroll.

Step 1 of 5

Ask your agent for a diagram

It reads the guide for that kind of diagram (there are 44) and writes a .fdraw file into your repository.

> Draw our checkout flow as docs/checkout.fdraw
Reading the guide for architecture diagrams
Writing docs/checkout.fdraw

Step 2 of 5

The agent runs the checker

fastdraw check lists overlapping boxes, text that overflows and lines that cross a box, each with a line number. The agent fixes those before it reports back.

$ fastdraw check docs/checkout.fdraw
ok: no problems found
scroll

Change the look
